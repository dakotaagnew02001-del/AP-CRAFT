# A&P Craft v5.0 — Expanded Ground Vehicle Catalog

- Added 24 new purchasable ground-vehicle catalog entries: classic VW Beetle, economy/family cars, motorcycles, sports cars, pickups, commercial trucks and rugged 4x4s.
- Kept all 37 pre-existing model IDs unchanged. New ground vehicles are IDs 38–61.
- Vehicle dealer now offers all ground models through clickable NPC chat; aircraft dealer continues to offer only aircraft.
- Purchases charge existing wallet, spawn owned rideable placeholder vehicles, and tag category/trail/heavy-hauler metadata for future systems.
- No custom 3D vehicle models, motorcycle physics, fuel, durability, towing or working passenger/cargo differentiation yet. All new models use vanilla boat placeholders.
- Source archive only. Compilation and in-game testing remain outstanding.
