# v5.5 — Portable work bags

Added three craft-creative-inventory registered portable storage items: Work Bag (9 slots), Tool Backpack (18 slots), and Expedition Backpack (27 slots). Right-click to open a vanilla chest-style inventory. Contents are stored in the bag's own NBT and persist with the item. Nested bags and shift-click transfers are restricted. This is a source prototype, not a compiled/tested release. Recipes, vendor integration, dedicated textures and full slot-locking are pending.
