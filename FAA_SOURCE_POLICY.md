# FAA-only source policy for A&P Craft lessons

New educational lessons must use primary FAA publications, not third-party prep-bank wording or unverified memory. Every lesson must name its FAA publication, topic/chapter, and the applicable Aviation Mechanic ACS subject. Source-linked material is paraphrased, not presented as an exact FAA quotation. In-world steps are game simulations, not FAA-issued procedures.

## Primary FAA sources
- FAA-H-8083-30B, Aviation Maintenance Technician Handbook—General
- FAA-H-8083-31B, Aviation Maintenance Technician Handbook—Airframe
- FAA-H-8083-32B, Aviation Maintenance Technician Handbook—Powerplant
- FAA-S-ACS-1, Aviation Mechanic General, Airframe, and Powerplant Airman Certification Standards
- 14 CFR Part 43, including section 43.9 for applicable maintenance record entries

Official handbook index: https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation
Official ACS PDF: https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/Aviation_Mechanic_Certification_Standards.pdf
Official Powerplant chapters: https://www.faa.gov/regulationspolicies/handbooksmanuals/aviation/faa-h-8083-32b-aviation-maintenance-technician

## Practical simulation safeguards
- No universal aircraft-specific torque, rigging, compression, or inspection limits.
- Actual aircraft work requires applicable manufacturer's maintenance instructions, approved/acceptable data, tooling, and qualifications.
- A text-based task acknowledgement is not a hands-on practical proficiency assessment.
- The existing /apcraft quiz, /work, /diagnose, and older training content predate this policy. They have NOT yet been audited item-by-item and must be labeled legacy/unverified until source mapped.
- Do not present generated questions as verbatim FAA exam questions.
